A walking plan turns a goal you are not ready for yet into a series of smaller ones. You pick what you are walking for, the app sets a target for that goal, and then it splits the gap between your current steps and the target into stages. Each stage becomes your [daily goal](/docs/getting-started/set-your-daily-goal) until you have hit it enough times to move up.

## How do I start a walking plan?

Open **Settings** in the app. Under **Daily Goal**, tap **Walking Plan**.

1. **Pick a goal.** Each one sets the target differently (see below).
2. **Check your current steps.** The slider starts at your average over the last two weeks, rounded to the nearest 250. If you move it, tap **Apply** to go back to the average.
3. **Fill in the goal's details.** Only some goals need them: age for a longer, healthier life; weight, height, and extra calories for weight loss; your steps before recovery for regaining your activity level. A button below them fills these in from Apple Health. The app asks for access to your age, weight, and height only here, never during setup.
4. **Review the plan.** The result shows the target, a staircase of stages from your base to the target, the minimum number of days the climb takes, and how many extra minutes of walking a day it asks for.
5. Tap **Create.**

Your daily goal switches to the first stage straight away.

## How is the target set?

| Goal                                   | Target                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Lose weight**                        | Your current steps plus enough extra steps to burn 150, 250, or 350 kcal a day |
| **Maintain weight loss**               | 8,500 steps, or your current steps if already higher                           |
| **Live a longer, healthier life**      | 8,000 steps under 60, 7,000 at 60 and over                                     |
| **Improve heart and metabolic health** | 7,000 steps                                                                    |
| **Mood**                               | 7,000 steps                                                                    |
| **Manage knee arthritis**              | 6,000 steps                                                                    |
| **Regain your activity level**         | Your usual steps before the surgery, injury, or illness                        |

Targets are rounded to the nearest 100 and capped at 20,000.

For weight loss, calories per step scale with your weight and stride, and stride is estimated from your height. A heavier, taller walker burns more per step and needs fewer of them. The plan also estimates the weekly loss those calories add up to, assuming your diet stays the same.

These are the same numbers as the web [step goal calculator](/tools/step-goal-calculator), which explains the research behind each one.

## How are the stages set?

Each stage is a little higher than the last, rounded to the nearest 100:

- **Most goals:** about 10% of your current steps, never less than 500 or more than 1,000 per stage.
- **Regain your activity level:** about 10% too, but between 250 and 500, so the climb is gentler.

For example, if you walk 5,000 steps a day and are 40, **Live a longer, healthier life** sets a target of 8,000. That gives five stages, 5,500, 6,000, 6,500, 7,000, and 7,500, and then the target itself. At five hit days per stage, that is at least 25 days.

If you already walk enough for the goal, there are no stages. The plan starts at the target and works on keeping it up.

## How do I move up a stage?

Hit the stage's goal on **5 days** and you move up. Those days do not have to be in a row. Only days you hit count, so a missed day does not reset the count, but it does not add to it either.

On the final stage, your fifth hit takes you to the target, and the plan switches from building to holding.

## What are skip days?

Skip days cover the days you miss.

- You start with **2 skip days**.
- A missed day uses one automatically. Your stage count is kept, but your streak resets.
- Every **3-day streak** of hits earns one back, up to a maximum of **3**.
- If you miss with **no skip days left**, you step back one stage and start that stage's count again. Your skip days refill to 2, so one bad week cannot slide you all the way down.

If you miss while holding your target, you step back to the last stage and climb back up from there.

## What counts as a hit?

A day is a hit if your total reaches that day's goal. The app judges each day once it has ended, using the day boundary you set in [The step window](/docs/steps-and-data/step-window). Until then, today's mark on the dots and bars shows at half strength.

A day with **no steps recorded at all** is logged as no data, not a miss. That usually means the phone was off or Health access was lost, so it costs no skip day and breaks no streak.

If you have not opened the app for a while, it catches up on the missing days the next time it runs, judging each one against the goal that applied that day.

## When does it become a habit?

Once you reach the target, the plan counts the days you hit it. At **66 days** at the target, the plan marks the habit as formed. Sixty-six days is the median time to form a habit found in a 2009 study by Lally and colleagues at University College London.

The plan keeps running after that. Your goal stays at the target, and skip days and streaks keep working the same way.

## Where can I see my progress?

Tap the plan summary in **Settings**. It shows:

- today's goal, the stage you are on, and how many more hit days you need to move up
- your skip days and your streak toward the next one
- the staircase, with your current stage highlighted
- the last 14 days, each marked hit, skipped, stepped back, or no data, along with milestones such as moving up a stage or earning a skip day
- the rules, as a reminder

The **Walking Plan** widget shows the same progress on the Home Screen in small and medium sizes, and on the Lock Screen and Apple Watch as circular, rectangular, and inline accessories. See [The widget gallery](/docs/widgets/widget-gallery) for how to add one.

## Can I change my daily goal while a plan is running?

Not directly. While a plan is running, it sets the daily goal: the **Daily Goal** row in Settings becomes the plan summary, and the goal changes on its own as you move between stages. Every widget, Live Activity, and [goal reminder](/docs/goal-reminders/how-goal-reminders-work) follows the current stage.

On Apple Watch, the Daily Goal setting shows the plan instead of a stepper. Plans are created, changed, and judged on iPhone only, because the watch have limited history data and tiny screens.

## How do I end or change a plan?

Open the plan and tap **End Plan**. Your stage, streak, and skip days are cleared, and your daily goal stays at whatever it was that day, so nothing jumps.

To switch to a different goal, end the current plan and create a new one.

## Does it work with Wheelchair Mode?

Walking Plan is built for steps: the targets and the calorie maths both assume walking. With **Wheelchair Mode** on, the Walking Plan entry is hidden from Settings. A plan you started before turning it on keeps running, with a note that its targets may not fit pushes.
