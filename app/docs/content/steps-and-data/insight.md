Insight is the app's long view: a month of your daily steps as a chart, one observation about your recent week, and a goal line you can drag to see how a different target would have worked out.

Open it from **Settings › Insight** — *"Your week, and the goal that fits."*

## The monthly chart

Each bar is a day. A horizontal line marks your daily goal, so which days cleared it is immediately visible. You can move between months, and the current month is where it opens.

## Dragging the goal line

**Drag the goal line** and the whole report re-evaluates against the draft value as you move it: the bars, the stats, and the count of days you cleared. Release it and the value becomes your new goal, applied everywhere.

This is more reliable than guessing at a round number, because you are choosing against a month of your own days: you can see whether 9,000 would have meant reaching the goal four times or twenty. The line moves in the same 200-step increments as the slider, between 2,200 and 20,000.

## The insight

Above the chart is one observation about your last seven days. There are seven kinds, and the app selects whichever is most notable:

| Insight | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Milestone** | A new all-time total since you started tracking |
| **Personal best** | Your highest single day, with how far back you had to look |
| **Best week** | Your biggest week, and how many weeks since the last one |
| **Goal days** | How many of the last seven days cleared your goal |
| **Steadiest** | Your most consistent week — no big days, no empty ones |
| **Strongest weekday** | Which day of the week is reliably yours |
| **Peak hour** | The hour you most often walk |

Rarity decides which one is shown. A personal best you have not beaten in over a year scores higher than one from last month.

The last three describe a pattern rather than a performance, so they are available whatever the week looked like. This means the card always has something to show: a quiet week gets *"You're a morning walker"* instead of a negative result.

## Sharing

Insight renders as an image you can share. There is also a **Messages** extension: in a conversation, open the Steps app from the iMessage app row and tap **Share Today's Steps** to send your current count and goal as a card.

If the Messages extension says it has no data, open the main app once so it can record today's steps.

## Does this replace the Health app?

No. The Health app holds the full history, every source, and every metric. Insight is narrower: it answers how your walking has been going recently and whether your goal is set at the right level.

## Where does the data come from?

Apple Health, read on your device, same as everything else. Insight computes the report locally each time you open it — nothing is uploaded, and there is no server involved in choosing your insight. See [Privacy and sync](/docs/steps-and-data/privacy-and-sync).
